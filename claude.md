# Stockroom Standard — System Architecture & Operational Playbook

> **Status: Concept draft.** Everything below — hardware specs, the universal mounting system, pricing formulas, and margins — is a starting point for partner discussion, not a finalized design. Treat specific numbers and mechanisms as illustrative until validated against real vendor quotes, prototypes, and installer time studies.

## Executive Summary & Core Concept
* **High-Concept Pitch:** Modular, structural pantry storage installations ranging from commercial-grade heavy-duty shelving to luxury, fully customized butler's-pantry suites, paired with an optional full-service concierge pantry reset.
* **Core Innovation:** Standardized structural hardware across all tiers anchored by a heavy-duty vertical wall-mounting track rated for restaurant-grade bulk loads, enabling seamless client upgrades from Tier 1 to Tier 3 over time without wall damage.
* **Market Opportunity:** Bridges the gap between cheap wire pantry shelving ($150–$400, sags under bulk/Costco-style loads) and hyper-expensive custom butler's-pantry cabinetry ($4,000–$12,000+ with long lead times).
* **Target ICP:** Large families and bulk shoppers, suburban homeowners, first-time home buyers (ages 28–45), rental property managers, and luxury homeowners wanting a butler's pantry or walk-in pantry suite.
* **Core Guarantee:** "Built to hold your bulk haul"—a lifetime anti-sag warranty on all structural shelving.

## Hardware Sourcing & Standardization Architecture

### Sourcing Strategy Matrix
* **Tier 1 (Heavy-Duty Wire Shelving):**
  * *Material:* NSF-certified commercial-grade chrome or epoxy-coated wire shelving, 14–18 gauge, in restaurant/kitchen shelving profiles.
  * *Sourcing:* Direct commercial accounts with foodservice equipment distributors (e.g., Metro, Regency, WebstaurantStore) at commercial-account pricing. Pre-sized in standard depths (12"/18"/24") to eliminate job-site cutting.
  * *Anchoring:* Adjustable posts secured to the universal wall track via locking brackets — no separate wall penetration per shelf.
  * *Wear-Point Hardware:* Locking brackets spec'd with nylon/Delrin sleeves at the post contact point (same commercial shelving families already sourced, no cost premium) rather than bare coated-metal-on-metal — this is standard on commercial wire shelving like ClosetMaid ShelfTrack and prevents chip-through at the point where shelves get repositioned.
* **Tier 2 (The Adjustable Steel System):**
  * *Material:* Powder-coated steel vertical standards, adjustable bracket arms, pull-out wire or composite drawer bins, door-mounted spice racks.
  * *Sourcing:* Commercial OEM storage suppliers (e.g., Rev-A-Shelf, Container Store Elfa-style component makers, Knape & Vogt) at 40–50% off retail.
  * *Wear-Point Hardware:* Bracket clips and pull-out bin rails spec'd with nylon/Delrin bushings at the contact/slide points to prevent powder-coat chip-through under repeated adjustment and use.
* **Tier 3 (The Butler's Suite):**
  * *Substrates:* 3/4" Thermally Fused Laminate (TFL) over industrial-grade MDF (80% of builds to eliminate on-site painting/finishing) or wood veneer/paint-grade for kitchen-matched cabinetry.
  * *Sourcing:* 4'x8' sheets via regional architectural panel suppliers; cabinetry hardware (soft-close hinges/slides) via commercial cabinet hardware distributors.

### Universal Mounting System (Upgrade Path)
* **Universal Vertical Standard Track:** 14-gauge cold-rolled steel continuous wall track mounted into every stud, floor to near-ceiling.
* **Tier 1 Attachment:** Wire shelving posts lock directly into the universal track using adjustable locking brackets — reconfigurable shelf heights without new holes.
* **Tier 2 Attachment:** Notched vertical standards and pull-out bin rails snap directly onto the universal track.
* **Tier 3 Attachment:** Structural MDF cabinet carcasses slot into the exact same track system, converting the space into a built-in butler's pantry without tearing out original wall attachments.

## Financial Models & Pricing Formulas

### Financial Target Metrics
* **Target Gross Margin:** Minimum **65%** overall across all tiers and services.
* **Target Labor Cost:** **15% – 18%** of total job price (sub-contracted or employee installer flat-rate payouts).

### Base Retail Pricing Formula
$$\text{Linear Foot Retail Price} = \frac{\text{Direct Material Cost/ft} + \text{Direct Labor Cost/ft}}{1 - \text{Target Gross Margin Percent}}$$

## Premium Accessories & Upgrades (Tier 3 Upsell)

* **Positioning:** High-margin, sales-floor upsell layered onto a Tier 3 Butler's Suite quote. Priced and sold as discrete line items, not baked into the linear-foot rate.
* **Sourcing Model:** Fulfilled through certified third-party appliance/hardware manufacturers rather than in-house fabrication — Stockroom Standard handles carpentry integration and install; the manufacturer's own warranty covers the unit itself.

### Liability & Compliance Protocol
* **Not Covered by Structural Warranty:** Appliances, fridges, and smart devices carry the manufacturer's own warranty; Stockroom Standard's Lifetime Structural Guarantee covers the surrounding shelving/cabinetry framing only.
* **Electrical Disclosure:** Any accessory requiring a dedicated outlet (wine fridge, powered lift shelf) requires a licensed electrician sign-off before install; not included in the accessory price.
* **Sales Script Cue:** Position as *"We build the space around it — the appliance itself comes from a certified manufacturer with its own warranty."*

## White-Glove Pantry Reset Concierge Integration

### Package Breakdown
* **Basic Reset:** Systematically pack pantry contents into totes, complete hardware installation, and restock items back by category.
* **Concierge Reset:** Full inventorying, expired-item purge support, professional zone-based organization (baking, snacks, breakfast, canned goods), matching airtight canisters and labeled bins, and old packaging/box removal.

### Two-Person Operational Protocol


[Arrival & Digital Pre-Audit] (Installer + Organizer)
│
▼
[Pack-Down & Pantry Staging] (Organizer Lead)
└── Mobile totes and coolers (for perishables) outside work zone
│
▼
[Tear-Out & Structural Hardware Build] (Installer Lead)
└── Remove old shelving, mount vertical track, assemble Tier 1-3 modules, cleanup
│
▼
[Restock & Professional Zone Organization] (Organizer Lead)
└── Transfer to matching canisters, label by zone, organize bulk/canned goods by category

### Risk & Liability Protocols
* **Perishables & Expiration Disclosure:** Organizer flags expired or spoiled items for client decision — does not discard without approval. Refrigerated/frozen items are excluded from the reset scope.
* **Pre-Existing Damage Audit:** High-resolution digital photo log taken of all existing pantry conditions prior to touching contents.

## Digital Quote Engine & Lead Conversion Architecture

### Calculator Logic Workflow
1. **User Inputs:** Wall length (linear feet), pantry shape (reach-in vs. walk-in), and current photos.
2. **Hardware Selection:** User toggles between Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 pricing.
3. **Service Add-On:** Dynamic checkbox for **Basic Reset** ($30/ft, min $300) or **Concierge Reset** ($65/ft, min $650).
4. **Formula Execution:** `Total Quote = (Selected Tier $/ft * Linear Ft) + MAX(Service Floor Price, Service $/ft * Linear Ft)`
5. **Output:** Instant 3D parametric preview + 3-tiered price matrix + $99 refundable deposit booking link.

## Lifetime Warranty Structure

* **Lifetime Structural Guarantee:** Covers wall anchoring integrity, shelving/track failure, and structural joinery for homeownership duration.
* **5-Year Mechanical Warranty:** Covers soft-close drawer slides, hinges, and edge-banding.
* **Weight Limits:** 250 lbs/linear foot for Tier 1 (commercial wire shelving rating); 150 lbs/linear foot for Tiers 2 & 3.
* **Coating Wear Exclusion:** Powder-coat/epoxy wear at moving contact points (bracket clips, drawer slide rails) is cosmetic only and excluded from the Lifetime Structural Guarantee — the underlying steel's load rating is unaffected. Field techs carry a touch-up powder-coat pen for warranty-visit spot repair.
